Reporting a security problem

Updated November 25, 2026

Found a hole? Tell us first.

  • Report it privately, with the exact steps or request that shows the problem.
  • Only test with your own accounts. Don't read, change or download other people's data.
  • Give us a fair chance to fix it before telling anyone else.

How to report

  • In the app: Settings → Report a Bug. Start the title with [Security] so it reaches the team quickly.
  • By email: support@heyzuno.uk, with [Security] at the start of the subject.
  • Or message the Zuno team in the Zuno Official server and ask to send a security report privately.

A good report has: what you found, where (the page or API address), the exact steps or request, what you expected, and what actually happened. Screenshots help.

Please don't

  • Run load or "benchmark" tests, floods or anything that slows Zuno down for other people.
  • Access, keep, change or share anyone else's account, messages or files.
  • Use social tricks on the Zuno team or other users, or post the problem publicly before it's fixed.

What we'll do

  • Look into every real report, and keep you posted.
  • Fix confirmed problems as fast as we can, and credit you if you'd like.
  • If you report in good faith and follow this page, we won't treat your research as an attack.
